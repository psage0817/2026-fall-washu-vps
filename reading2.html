The New Turing Test

This is a reading response to the article, The New Turing Test.

I found this article super interesting because it reframes the question that is in many people’s brains about AI. As detecting AI work gets harder and harder, the more appropriate way to decipher between human made and AI generated work is to question if there is humanity behind the work. The previous test made by Alan Turing doesn’t really work anymore, since AI has evolved to mimic human communication almost identically. However, since AI is a statistical average of a human database, noticing specificities, individual identity, and specific reasoning behind work can distinguish it as a human form of expression rather than an AI generated piece. Instead of asking about the capabilities of computers, it’s important to try and expand the possibilities of humans.
