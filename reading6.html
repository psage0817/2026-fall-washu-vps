There are no gardens on the internet

This is a reading response to Hyundai Artlab website made by Chia Amisola
At first, I noticed how this website was extremely textured and colorful, yet it didn't feel particularly busy or messy, at least not to the point where it was overly distracting. The website felt like a cohesive blend between digital/interactive mediums and analog textures and colors. The way the website was colored reminded me of heavy paint or even infrared cameras. The interactive experience itself was very captivating and walked the viewer through a story with just the simple click of a button. In this way, the viewer stays on the website for longer and becomes more deeply immersed in the world of the site. In a way, the website felt more like an art installation or an abstraction of a website than a traditional regular site typically visited on the internet. That made the experience feel more special and unique.
