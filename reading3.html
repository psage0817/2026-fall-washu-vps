My Website is a Shifting House

This is a reading response to the article, "My Website is a Shifting House.""

1. What did you think about the essay? Did anything in particular resonate? I think it's certainly interesting that websites have grounded themselves in the technological society as information hubs. This has led people to forget that websites can exist for any reason, and it can just be a special space on the internet. The argument that websites can have their own personality and can evolve over time was also super interesting to me, as someone who has a portfolio website that I want to update over time.

2. Laurel proposes a website could be a river, a garden, a thrown rock. If you have a website, what could your website be? I think my website would be akin to a room or studio space of my own. I like to think of my website as being a space where I have full creative control and can decorate it to my liking.
3. What can you do with a website that you make yourself that you can’t do if someone else makes your website? When I make a website, I can have full control over the interactions, the layouts, and everything about it that the person on the other side of the screen will experience. If someone else makes my website, I have no control over what the experience and interactions will be, and they may not fit what I had in mind that fits my brand the best. It would be like having somebody else make a painting about my life for me.
