Brian Eno documentary film

This is a reading response to the Brian Eno documentary film
This was a really interesting film to watch because of how abstract the film itself was on top of it being a documentary. Though this was about the artist Brian Eno and his artistic process, because he is a media artist, it made sense for the documentary itself to also have abstract and artistic elements that often were jarring and seemed like it came from nowhere. In particular, his process where he described having both a child and a critic in his brain felt very relatable and honest. When working creatively, it is important to know when to be experimental without any boundaries, and when to be focused to lock important details down. Finding this balance is a meticulous process that he described very well with his chosen metaphor.
