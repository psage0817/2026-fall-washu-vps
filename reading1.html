Why Restaurant Websites

I think the lack of restaurant websites reflects the phenomenon where many restaurants are losing their identities as third spaces, rather than just a place to eat. I've noticed this in places like Panera and Starbucks that used to have solidified branding and a functionality as a third space according to the branding, but since they've evolved as corporations, the priority is to just cycle through as much customers as possible. Perhaps the lack of restaurant websites reflects the fact that restaurants (especially ones owned by corporations) don't think these spaces reflecting branding and community are worth investing time into creating anymore. However, good branding and a good restaurant website can be the difference between whether someone chooses to go to that restaurant or not. Restaurant websites ARE worth the time and energy to create, and it's a shame that they've become so rare.
