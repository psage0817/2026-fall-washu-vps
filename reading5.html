The Forbidden Zone

This is a reading response to the Forbidden Zone
I tend to find inspiration from a plethora of experiences, other artwork that I encounter, and even forces of nature and philosophy. This article provided a deep explanation and thought to this process, and I found myself thinking of my own inspirations while reading. Even the organization of information and the thoughtful gathering of information is a creative process itself, yet it doesn't have to have a clear direction or serve a meaningful purpose other than for my own insight. Though it is natural and recommended to have inspirations and references, the article also points out the blurry line between using something as a reference and using it as your own, and often finding this balance can be difficult. Artwork doesn't exist in a plane; everything is inspired by everything else, but designers can't exist within other designers. They must find and take up their own space in the industry.
